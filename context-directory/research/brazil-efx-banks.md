# eFX banks in Brazil — counterparties for Unlimit Brasil

Last updated: 2026-10-06
Prepared for: Unlimit Brasil, authorised payment institution collecting for
foreign merchants

---

## Read this before the list

**eFX is a defined regulatory term in Brazil, not a market nickname.** It is the
*serviço de pagamento ou transferência internacional* created by Resolução BCB
277/2022 under Lei 14.286/2021. It covers exactly our shape of business: a
Brazilian consumer pays in reais for goods or services sold by a non-resident,
and the provider converts and remits. That is why the question "which banks do
eFX" has a real answer and not just a list of banks with an FX desk.

**The rules changed the day before yesterday.** Resolução BCB 561/2026,
published 30 April 2026, took effect **1 October 2026**. Four changes matter
to us:

1. eFX may now only be provided by institutions authorised by the BCB.
   Unauthorised providers have until **31 May 2027** to file for authorisation.
2. Client funds in transit for eFX must sit in **segregated accounts**.
3. Settlement between the Brazilian provider and its counterpart abroad must go
   through an FX operation or a non-resident real account held in Brazil.
   **Virtual assets, stablecoins included, are banned as a settlement means.**
   This is what closed Banco Genial's eFX service for our crypto merchants.
4. Scope widens to cover financial and capital-market investment transfers up
   to **USD 10,000** per transaction.

## The question that comes before choosing a bank

**Unlimit Brasil may not need an eFX bank at all. It may be able to provide eFX
itself.**

Under Res. BCB 561/2026, an authorised payment institution can provide eFX
directly — *without* a separate authorisation to operate in the FX market — if
it is authorised in one of three modalities:

- emissor de moeda eletrônica
- emissor de instrumento de pagamento pós-pago
- credenciador (acquirer)

Unlimit Brasil is an authorised IP. **Which modality is it authorised in?** If
it is a credenciador or an e-money issuer, the bank search below is about
pricing and reach, not about permission. If it is something else — an
*iniciador de transação de pagamento*, for instance — then a bank is mandatory
and the list is the whole answer.

> **Deadline, 28 days out.** Institutions already authorised that provide eFX
> must declare that service in their **Unicad** registration by
> **30 October 2026**. If Unlimit Brasil is providing eFX today and this has
> not been filed, that is the most urgent item on this page — more urgent than
> any counterparty conversation.

## Tier 1 — Bancos de câmbio (the exclusive FX bank licence)

A *banco de câmbio* is a distinct BCB licence category: it may only do FX and
FX-linked credit. These are the institutions whose entire business is this, and
they are where the eFX desks for payment facilitators actually sit.

## What to ask on the first call

The five questions from the FX broker work still apply — binding rate or
indicative, how long a rate holds, locks per day, caps on the outward transfer,
purpose code for the pooled transfer to the UAE entity. On top of those, four
that are specific to eFX in Brazil:

1. **Does your eFX account accept funds from a third-party payment processor,
   or only directly from end clients and card acquirers?** Genial says the
   latter. If that is the market standard, our pooled model has a structural
   problem, not a commercial one — ask this first, before pricing.
2. How do you handle the **segregated account** requirement for eFX client
   funds in transit under Res. 561, and what does it cost us operationally?
3. Settlement with the counterpart abroad has to be an FX operation or a
   non-resident real account held in Brazil. **Which of the two do you use for
   a transfer to a UAE entity**, and what does that do to timing?
4. We serve crypto on-ramp and off-ramp merchants. **After 1 October 2026, what
   is your position on them?** Genial has already withdrawn. Getting this
   answer up front avoids repeating that experience.

And one that decides the shape of the whole project: ask each of them whether,
in their reading, **Unlimit Brasil's own IP authorisation lets it provide eFX
directly**. They deal with this question weekly. Their answers are free, and
they will disagree with each other in instructive ways.

## Method and limits

Built on 2 October 2026 from public sources via web search. Bank websites,
bcb.gov.br and most Brazilian corporate domains are blocked by this
environment's egress proxy, so **nothing here was read from a bank's own site
directly** — contacts come from search results, directories, ABRACAM listings
and press coverage, and should be treated as leads to verify, not as confirmed
desk numbers. Several addresses are general, ombudsman or procurement channels
and are marked where known.

This list is not provably complete. The authoritative source is the Central
Bank's own register of institutions authorised to operate in the FX market, at
bcb.gov.br/?INSTCRED, which should be pulled by someone with unrestricted
network access before this is treated as final. The eFX-specific population
will also become visible through Unicad once the 30 October 2026 declaration
deadline passes.
